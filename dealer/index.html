In the Finti application, please provide all documentation necessary for the lenders you selected. Incomplete files slow lender review.

Use the prep checklist your rep sent (Dealer Application Ready Checklist) so every document and detail your application asks for is ready the first time.

One important timing note

Activation waits until every lender you applied with has Approved or Declined. We don’t move to rates, setup, or Live on a partial set — that keeps everything clean and ready when you go live.

Your journey

Six steps from first packet to Live. Your rep is with you the whole way.

  1. 1

    You receive your Finti application

    You + your rep

    Your Finti rep sends your application and a prep checklist so you know what to gather before you start.

    Ready when: You have the application and the checklist.
  2. 2

    You complete the application & upload documents

    Your key step

    Fill out the form and upload every document and detail the application asks for for the lenders you selected. This is the step that keeps everything on track — incomplete uploads delay lender review.

    Ready when: Nothing is missing for the lenders you chose.
  3. 3

    We submit your file to lenders

    We handle this

    Once your application is complete, we submit it to the lenders you selected.

    Ready when: Your file is with those lenders.
  4. 4

    Lenders review

    ~7–10 days avg

    Lenders typically take about 7–10 days on average. Timing can vary by lender — we track responses while you wait.

    Ready when: Decisions are coming in.
  5. 5

    Full decisions, then rates & setup

    Hard gate · then we set you up

    We wait until every lender you applied with has Approved or Declined. Then we set you up with your approved options, share rate information, you pick your rates, and we load them on your location.

    Ready when: Your approved rates are on your location.
  6. 6

    Training, then you’re Live

    You’re Live

    We schedule training on the Finti process and portal. After that, you’re Live — ready to sell with Finti.

    Ready when: You’re trained and can originate through Finti.

Questions?

Your Finti rep is your first point of contact.

They’ll send the prep checklist, help with the application, and keep you posted as lenders respond. Prefer email? Reach us through getfinti.com.
