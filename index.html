Finti Onboarding Procedure

How a dealer moves from first application packet to Live — approved lenders on location, rates loaded, trained, and selling with Finti.

10steps end to end
~7–10 daysavg lender turnaround
Full setApprove or Decline before activate

Hard gate

Dealers are not activated until every applicable lender has responded — Approve or Decline. Partial lender approvals do not unlock activation, rate-sheet send, or Live.

Friday activations (for now)

For now (until operations scale): do not activate any dealer live to sell on a Friday.

Purpose

One shared playbook for Finti reps, MoM, Brittany, Shawn, and corporate — so every dealer gets the same path from packet to Live.

The procedure

Follow in order. Owner chips show who drives each step.

  1. 1

    Application packet out

    Finti Rep

    Finti Rep sends the dealer application and the Dealer onboarding PDF (Dealer Application Ready Checklist).

    Done when: Dealer has the application and the PDF.
  2. 2

    Dealer completes application

    Dealer

    Dealer fills out the form and uploads all documents and information required for the lenders selected. Rep supports as needed.

    Done when: Application is complete for every selected lender; MoM can submit without chasing missing docs.
  3. 3

    Submit to lenders

    MoM

    MoM (Evan and team) submits the completed application to all applicable lenders for that dealer.

    Done when: Every selected lender has an open submission.
  4. 4

    Wait for lender decisions

    MoM tracks

    Average turnaround from application: ~7–10 days. MoM monitors responses while lenders decide.

    Done when: Clock is running and responses are being tracked.
  5. 5

    Full lender response set

    MoM · Gate

    Lenders may approve at different times. Do not activate until every applicable lender has responded in the affirmative or with a decline.

    Done when: Response set is complete (every lender = Approve or Decline). Partial approvals stay parked.
  6. 6

    Add approved lenders + send rate sheets

    MoM · Brittany C

    All approved lenders are added to the dealer location. Brittany C sends rate sheets for those lenders.

    Done when: Approved lenders are on the location and rate sheets are with the dealer.
  7. 7

    Dealer picks approved rates

    Dealer

    After rate sheets are sent, the dealer picks their approved rates.

    Done when: Dealer’s rate selections are confirmed back to Brittany / MoM.
  8. 8

    Upload rates to location

    Brittany · Evan

    Brittany and Evan upload all approved (selected) rates into the dealer’s location(s) in the portal.

    Done when: Live location pricing matches what the dealer picked.
  9. 9

    Training scheduled

    Evan · Shawn

    Schedule training on the Finti process and the portal with the dealer, Evan, and Shawn.

    Done when: Training date is booked.
  10. 10

    Dealer is Live

    MoM confirms

    Dealer is Live to sell with Finti — location active, rates loaded, trained, and set up to succeed.

    Done when: Dealer can originate through Finti.
